# The Slab assumes a right hand, provisionally

The Slab is laid out for a right thumb and there is no handedness setting.

This is recorded as provisional on purpose. It is the decision in the Slab design most likely to
change, because it is the one that cannot be settled on paper - it needs the app held one-handed on
the device it is built for, a Samsung S23. Mirroring the layout is a few lines if the hand disagrees
with the drawing.

## Considered Options

- **A handedness setting.** Rejected for now. A setting is a permanent answer to a question that has
  not been asked yet by anyone, on an app with one known user, and settings are easy to add and hard
  to remove.

## Consequences

Revisiting this needs a phone in a hand, not another discussion. Until then, do not treat the
right-handed layout as load-bearing: nothing else in the design should come to depend on which side
a control sits.
